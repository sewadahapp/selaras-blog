The first time I played a room with only twenty chairs, I thought the small
audience would make the performance easier. It did the opposite. Every pause
became visible. Every quiet note had somewhere to go.

## Making space between songs

That intimacy changed the way I arrange live sets. I now leave more space
between songs, keep the transitions simple, and let the room contribute its
own texture.

![Rain beading on a window with blurred lights behind it at night. Photograph by Defi22, CC BY-SA 4.0.](/images/rainy-lights.jpg)

## What everyone listens to

> A performance is not just what is played. It is also what everyone agrees to
> listen to together.
