Most of the ideas that survive start as something small: a phrase overheard
on a night walk, the rhythm of a shutter door, a chord that shows up once and
never returns. The notebook exists so those things are not lost by morning.

## Write it down before it becomes a song

The rule is simple: write the observation first, the music second. A line
like "the rain made the neon louder" is worth more at three in the morning
than a finished loop that explains itself.

![City lights seen from the hills above Bandung at night. Photograph by chrisinno, CC BY 2.0.](/images/bandung-night-view-dago.jpg)

## The almost-miss is the point

Not every entry becomes something. That is fine. The notebook is less an
archive of songs than a record of attention — proof that the walk home was
listened to.
