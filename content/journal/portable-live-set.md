My ideal live setup fits into one backpack and can be ready in under ten
minutes. That constraint has made the music better, not smaller.

## Three ideas for a portable setup

The current setup is built around three ideas:

- Keep the signal path understandable.
- Choose tools that invite improvisation.
- Leave one part of the arrangement deliberately unfinished.

Most of it is a small semi-modular synth and a sampler. The synth plays the
bass and the pulse; the sampler holds the field recordings.

![A Moog DFAM and Mother-32 semi-modular synthesizer on a desk. Photograph via Wikimedia Commons, CC0.](/images/moog-drummer-from-another-mother-dfam-mother.jpg)

## Leave somewhere to go

The last point matters most. A live set should have somewhere to go.
