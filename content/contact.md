For bookings, collaborations, commissions, and press, the button above is the
real route — it reaches
[hello@naya-arunika.example](mailto:hello@naya-arunika.example), the same
inbox I actually read.

The studio sits in the Dago hills, and most good messages arrive the same way
as most good sounds: late, quiet, and one at a time.

![Bandung's station platforms late in the evening, lights on and mostly empty. Photograph by Midori, CC BY 3.0.](/images/bandung-station-platform.jpg)

## What to include

- A little about the project, and the stage it is at
- Your preferred timeline
- The kind of collaboration you have in mind
- A link to your work, venue, or organization

One link and one clear idea beat a full dossier.

![Asia-Afrika street in Bandung at night, lit shopfronts and glowing signs. Photograph by RasyaAbhirama13, CC BY-SA 4.0.](/images/night-at-asia-afrika-street-bandung.jpg)

## After you write

I usually reply within a few days — if a message seems to have vanished,
send it once more before assuming the worst. And if you would rather meet the
work before the person, start in the journal.
