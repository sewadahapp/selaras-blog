An original score for a short film about memory, distance, and returning home.
The score combines prepared piano, tape loops, and a small ensemble of found
percussion.

![Dago hills overlooking the lights of Bandung at dusk. Photograph by chrisinno, CC BY 2.0.](/images/bandung-night-view-dago.jpg)

The cue sheet was built backward: first the sound of every room the story
happens in, then the pitch each space already suggests — nothing plays over a
close-up that the room would not itself have answered.
