*Field Notes* is a sound installation built from recordings gathered across
West Java: a market opening before sunrise, rain on a tin roof, and the soft
rhythm of a train crossing a bridge.

![Night market lights on Asia-Afrika street, Bandung. Photograph by RasyaAbhirama13, CC BY-SA 4.0.](/images/night-at-asia-afrika-street-bandung.jpg)

![Bandung's station platforms late in the evening, lights on and mostly empty. Photograph by Midori, CC BY 3.0.](/images/bandung-station-platform.jpg)

The work asks visitors to slow down and listen for the geography inside an
ordinary day.

![City lights seen from the hills above Bandung at night. Photograph by chrisinno, CC BY 2.0.](/images/bandung-night-view-dago.jpg)

For one month the theatre stayed open after dark, the speakers left running,
so passersby on the square could hear the recordings migrate through the
building as the night cooled.
