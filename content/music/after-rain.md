*After Rain* is a five-track study in release and renewal. It was recorded
between a small bedroom studio and the back room of a Bandung coffee shop
during the wet season.

![Night market lights on Asia-Afrika street, Bandung. Photograph by RasyaAbhirama13, CC BY-SA 4.0.](/images/night-at-asia-afrika-street-bandung.jpg)

The record follows the feeling of a city becoming audible again after the rain:
motorbikes in the distance, water moving through gutters, and the first voices
returning to the street.

The ending track was cut last, at four in the morning, in one take — the
microphone left open so the first motorbikes outside became part of the song.
