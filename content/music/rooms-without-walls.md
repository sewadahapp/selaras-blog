A collection of live electronics and environmental recordings shaped by
stations, rooftops, and rooms that were never meant to be studios.

![A commuter train waiting under the lights of Gedebage Station at night. Photograph by NFarras, CC BY-SA 4.0.](/images/commuter-line-bandung-raya-in-gedebage-station.jpg)

Most of it was captured on a handheld recorder during ordinary trips across
Bandung, then assembled at home with a single synth and whatever the room
leaked in.

![A Moog DFAM and Mother-32 semi-modular synthesizer on a desk. Photograph via Wikimedia Commons, CC0.](/images/moog-drummer-from-another-mother-dfam-mother.jpg)
