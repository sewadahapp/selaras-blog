A short release for long-distance days. The title track began as a voice memo
and became a song about the small rituals that keep people close.

![City lights seen from the hills above Bandung at night. Photograph by chrisinno, CC BY 2.0.](/images/bandung-night-view-dago.jpg)

It was finished across three cities, ten thousand kilometres of broadband, and
one long night of mixing on borrowed monitors.
