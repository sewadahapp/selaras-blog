# Music for quiet places and unfinished thoughts

Naya Arunika is an independent musician, producer, and sound artist exploring
ambient electronics, intimate songwriting, and the sounds of everyday life.

> The best songs are sometimes found in the spaces between everything else.

## Latest from the studio

I make records slowly, collect field recordings from ordinary places, and
write about the process in the journal. The full musician portfolio will grow
from this Markdown-first foundation.
